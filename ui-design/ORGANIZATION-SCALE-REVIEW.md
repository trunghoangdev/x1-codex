# Larger organization scenario

Open Demos → Explore larger organization (`#/demos/organization`). This independent fictional scenario has six parallel workstreams across three projects, nine human/AI/deterministic workers, nineteen scoped bindings and eighteen assignments. It is a coordination design exercise, not live workload data or a new inbox for Alex.

The scenario gives multiple people assessment/authority responsibilities, allocates contributions to two AI workers and leaves an invitation review unassigned. Accessible onboarding demonstrates a revision loop; no earlier assessment transfers to a revised subject. Planner and executor bindings have no linked assignment in this bounded fixture. That does not establish availability or idle capacity.

Explore:

- Select workstreams to compare goals and three current responsibilities per stream.
- Search the assignment directory and combine worker/state filters.
- Select Unassigned to find the invitation gap, or Codex worker + Revision requested to find the onboarding loop.
- Inspect worker role scopes separately from assignment states.

States, participants and relationships are authored examples. No prerequisite readiness, delivery, authorization result or outcome is verified. The page records no responses, grants no permissions and leaves the five-assignment personal fixture intact. Filters and selection reset on refresh or leaving this demo.

Findings from this slice:

- A six-card stream overview fits desktop and stacks on mobile; explicit stream selection exposes its responsibility context.
- Worker/state filters make multi-assignee work searchable without treating a personal queue as an organization backlog.
- Eighteen assignment rows and nine worker entries still create a long mobile page. The primary next layout improvement is dedicated directories or paging, rather than adding all this content to Organization Overview.
- Current organization counts, fixture memberships and detail views still use the small main scenario. This independent experiment does not make those views support interchangeable organizations; a future scenario-provider model would be needed for that.

Validation covers mobile/desktop widths, composed filters, empty results, stream-selection focus, revision/gap context, scenario re-entry and unchanged personal assignments. No throughput or production performance claim is made.
