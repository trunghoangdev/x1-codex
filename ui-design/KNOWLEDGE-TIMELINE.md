# Unified Knowledge timeline

Open **Open Knowledge timeline** from Organization, My Work, K-01/K-02, contribution, attention or activity. The timeline projects retained local Knowledge records into one view: responsibility offers/responses, exact input/authority handoffs, contribution commands/admissions/deliveries, Maya’s receipts/assessments, scope adoption/applicability, bounded-use cycles and K-02 brief deliveries/receipts.

Each event identifies its actor, recorded time, workstream, version, record ID and stored references. Select an event to inspect its retained record and, where represented, its exact frozen source. References navigate to related timeline records; the source button opens the responsible contribution, reviewer, scope, use or workshop workspace. Historical delivery authors remain unchanged after handoff. Older use cycles retain their source and state; a source different from the current assessed contribution is labelled explicitly rather than receiving current authority.

Filters cover workstream, stage and text search across people, IDs, versions, decisions, rationale and references. Results are paginated in groups of twenty. Filter/page/selection state is represented in the URL. An unavailable selected record after workspace replacement is explained without inventing a replacement. Restoration/import rebuilds the projection from the checkpoint; no new persistence format or duplicate event store is added.

Newest recorded times appear first. Equal timestamps retain deterministic display order and do not prove causal order; record references describe represented relationships. Command submission rows show their current command status and explicitly warn that earlier status transitions have no separate recorded times. Admission is shown only when an admission timestamp exists. Receipt, assessment, authorization, execution and outcome remain separate events.

Unsent preparation, inferred missing steps, recovery operations and unrecorded transitions are not emitted. Authored activity examples remain accessible separately. Workshop delivery identifiers are derived from retained brief versions, not backend-generated event IDs. This timeline is a local record projection, not a production audit log, live stream or proof of outcome.

Validation covers exact actor/reference projection, both use cycles, stale source history, deterministic recovery, uncertain commands, filters/pagination, related-record/source navigation, phone/desktop layout and explicit whole-workspace restoration.
