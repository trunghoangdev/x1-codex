# Organization landing-page consolidation

Iteration 103 reorganizes existing content without changing source records or command/evidence semantics.

The primary order is organization purpose, attention summary and section navigation, compact Knowledge coordination needs, then workstream coordination. Case/decision/activity entrances follow the workstream section rather than preceding shared purpose or next actions. Main and larger scenarios retain their existing workstream representations.

Knowledge contribution exchange history and agreements/reviews/policy now use separate, clearly named disclosures after workstreams. Both are collapsed initially. The policy shortcut opens enclosing disclosures before focusing and scrolling to its heading, so keyboard navigation never targets hidden content. Contribution history can be opened by its summary using keyboard or pointer. Returning to the overview remounts these disclosures collapsed; the selected persona, source route and filters keep their existing behavior.

The contribution/receiver records remain unchanged and exact version history is still inspectable. The compact coordination list continues to open the correct receiver or contributor context. Sample boundaries, unknown follow-up ownership and independent outcome gaps remain intact. No feature is promoted to live data or server-authorized operation.

Validation targets desktop/phone ordering, closed initial details, policy shortcut focus, keyboard history disclosure, horizontal reflow, complete contributor/receiver revision loops, policy/source return, collaboration walkthrough and deferred loading/error recovery.
