# Exact assessed result to bounded use and outcome

Knowledge K-01 workstream, outcome and decision views now share a local exercise that continues the existing draft-02 contribution path. First deliver and receive draft-02 and record Maya's reassessment as **Suitable for stated scope**. A revision request, preparation, delivery or receipt alone cannot enter this use chain.

## Separate responsibilities and records

1. The demo organization owner records a scoped mandate, naming the internal preview audience and rationale. It explicitly designates Sam as this exercise's publication reviewer and bounded-use authorizer and Maya as its outcome reviewer. This is a simulated mandate, not an inferred permission from Maya's editorial role or a new production worker.
2. Sam separately assesses exact draft-02 against publication scope, recording suitability or revision needed with rationale. Revision needed stops the chain.
3. Sam separately allows or refuses use in the fictional internal preview environment. Refusal creates no execution.
4. Leo records a simulated execution observation, including success/failure and limitations. No publishing, dispatch or messages occur.
5. After success, Leo records a separate simulated reader observation or explicitly records missing reader evidence. Execution success alone is not usefulness evidence.
6. Maya reviews criterion K-01-goal against those records and records insufficient evidence, criterion not met, or criterion met in simulation. The positive conclusion requires successful execution and a represented simulated reader observation. Failed execution can still receive an insufficient/not-met review.

Every stage requires rationale and prepare/confirm/cancel controls. Prior records are immutable and linked by source IDs. Outcome review retains the criterion, exact subject, audience and prior observation chain. Actor labels explain which demo responsibility each action represents; selected persona does not enforce permissions.

## Exact source and scope boundaries

The mandate freezes the delivered text, scope note, input, receipt and editorial reassessment in an exact source snapshot. If a contribution restore/import removes or changes this subject, continuation is blocked and existing records remain inspectable. It cannot authorize a replacement guide using an earlier assessment. Returning to the exact source restores the represented linkage; no automatic record migration occurs.

The bounded mandate addresses publication responsibility only for this exercise. Original organization-wide publication gaps and authored counters remain context, not verified closure. Adopted agreement applicability remains unknown; no automatic adoption/authorization linkage is asserted. The authored standalone outcome example remains separate.

This single-cycle exercise deliberately does not support retry, mandate replacement, revocation or a later revision after refusal/failure. Those need new linked records and authority rules. It is local session state, excluded from contribution checkpoints and main snapshots, and clears on reload. Simulated reader evidence does not establish real customer outcomes.
