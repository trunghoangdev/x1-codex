# Outcome → organizational goal → next work

Iteration 140 adds a separate owner decision after the retained K-01 outcome review. K-01-goal is to help members find reliable answers and identify next steps. Every decision applies only to the exact guide, audience and use cycle; real-world and organization-wide success remain unverified.

## Owner decision

Open Knowledge's bounded-use view and inspect **Outcome → organizational goal → next work**. The owner records rationale and scope limits, then explicitly reviews and confirms one decision:

- **Goal met in simulation** is available only after an outcome of Criterion met in simulation. It creates no follow-up task and makes no claim about real readers.
- **Evidence gap** requires a follow-up title, recipient and expected result/acceptance criterion.
- **Further work required** also requires an explicit follow-up allocation.

Earlier goal decisions and the original outcome stay unchanged. New decisions link the previous goal review and exact outcome. Shared K-01 workstream/outcome views show the scoped local status; My Work identifies the next owner or recipient action.

## Follow-up work

1. The owner offers a bounded task to Leo, Maya or the separate demo outcome reviewer. These are local principals, not new production memberships or permission grants.
2. The recipient inspects the exact goal, outcome, task criterion and scope limits, acknowledges them and accepts. Clarification or decline preserves the original offer and returns planning to the owner.
3. Only the accepted recipient can deliver a result, including limitations. Delivery does not complete the task or revise the original outcome.
4. The owner separately reviews the result as Accepted result or Revision needed. A revised allocation requires a new goal decision/offer, preserving the previous delivery and review.
5. After task review, the owner reassesses the goal or plans a separately authorized use cycle/material. An accepted task result cannot turn an insufficient-evidence outcome into goal attainment. A fresh formal outcome review is required for new outcome conclusions.

The owner can explicitly cancel an open task with rationale. Acceptance, delivery and new goal decisions require the current exact assessed source. If the guide changes or enters revision, the task remains visible but cannot be reused for the new source; cancellation is still available. Complete/review or cancel open follow-up before starting another material mandate or use cycle.

## Retention and limits

The Knowledge timeline links goal decision → offer → response → delivery → owner review/cancellation. Whole-workspace checkpoint v10 validates exact source, outcome/criterion links, actors, acknowledgment, chronology and task transitions while reading earlier formats. Original cycles/materials retain their goal histories, and fresh cycles start without inherited goal attainment or tasks. Contribution-only recovery preserves this history and may leave its source stale.

At most ten owner goal reviews, with one follow-up each, are represented per cycle. If the bound is reached, unresolved gaps remain explicit; no automatic closure is inferred. All actions and results are local simulations, without messages, real reader observations or execution of the offered task.
