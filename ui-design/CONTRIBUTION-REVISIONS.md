# Requested content revisions

In Leo’s contribution workspace, **Further revision needed** exposes **Prepare draft-03**, then the corresponding next version through draft-09. A new draft copies the previous delivered text, clears the revision response and citation, and requires a fresh review and submission. Suitable assessments do not create another revision request. At draft-09 the UI explains the local limit.

Maya receives each new delivery separately and records a new immutable reassessment against its exact receipt and delivery. Every revision responds to the immediately preceding request: draft-03 responds to `human-reassessment-v2`, not the original draft-01 assessment. Earlier text, commands and decisions remain available in history. Pending/unknown commands and admission/projection lag retain the existing submission locks.

The comparison shows the latest two versions. Contributor preparation is visible to Leo; receiver/shared comparison waits for the new delivery. Shared progress and personal attention derive from the current version and its assessment.

Contribution checkpoint v3 supports up to nine consecutive versions and validates predecessor requests, command payloads and exact delivery/receipt/assessment links. Existing v1/v2 files remain readable. Whole Knowledge checkpoint and partial imports preserve the extended history without deleting other workspace slices.

Publication and outcome remain separate. Assessed draft-02 through draft-09 can receive fresh material mandates; earlier authorization never transfers. See [fresh material use](MATERIAL-USE-REVISIONS.md).

Validation covers repeated revisions through the limit, invalid lineage and mismatched receipts, checkpoint round trips, stale authorization blocking, and Leo/Maya interaction at phone and desktop widths. All records remain local samples; no backend or real participant result is claimed.
