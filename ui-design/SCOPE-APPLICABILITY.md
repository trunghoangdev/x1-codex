# Exact scope applicability decisions

Knowledge K-01 agreement pages now support explicit local decisions linking represented source records to the **current adopted scope**, not merely the selected proposal. A decision records adoption ID, agreement version, named audience, source kind/ID/frozen snapshot, simulated reviewer, rationale, timestamp and conclusion.

## Sources and decisions

The selector offers only represented records: fixed input-access-brief-v1, current delivered contribution, current assessment, bounded-use mandate/audience and a simulated reader observation when present. The assessment snapshot includes its exact delivery and receipt so reused record IDs cannot silently transfer applicability to changed material. The workshop K-02 brief remains separate.

Choose Applicable, Needs reassessment or Insufficient information, enter rationale, inspect the confirmation and record it locally. The input check acts as Leo, contribution/assessment/evidence checks as Maya, and bounded-use audience mapping as Sam. These are explicitly labelled simulated review responsibilities; they do not enforce authority or create production membership.

Decisions append history. The latest decision matching the **exact adoption, audience and source snapshot** governs current presentation. A newer adoption or changed source begins unknown; earlier decisions remain inspectable with their original context. A corrected decision appends another record instead of overwriting history. Missing records cannot be assessed through this form. Assignment-level impact remains a checklist until an exact record exists.

## Use boundaries

When an adopted scope exists, proceeding to use authorization or execution requires Applicable decisions for the exact editorial assessment and the bounded-use mandate/audience mapping. Missing mappings, Needs reassessment and Insufficient information show the precise source/adoption boundary and block those controls. Shared progress, attention and personal responsibilities expose the same condition and the relevant demo reviewer. The use detail links to applicability inspection.

Applicability does not create an authorization, change its audience or permit use. An existing allowed-use decision still needs its own record and is not rewritten when scope changes. Input, delivery and reader-evidence checks remain separately inspectable; they are not silently inferred from assessment applicability. Execution already observed remains historical, and outcome review still concerns its original bounded subject/audience. Applying those observations to a new scope requires an explicit evidence applicability decision; no organization-wide success is inferred.

Without an adopted agreement, the previously supported independent bounded-use exercise remains available and makes no agreement applicability claim. Further revisions, renewed authority and subsequent execution cycles remain future work.

## Recovery

Whole Knowledge checkpoints containing decisions use `forge.knowledge-workspace.v2`; existing v1 files remain readable. Empty applicability history exports v1 without the new field. The browser storage key is unchanged. Older UI versions do not understand v2.

Validation checks every decision's referenced adoption and replays the decision from a validated frozen contribution/use context. Unknown fields, forged reviewer/source links, foreign adoption IDs and v1 files carrying decisions are rejected. Up to 100 decisions fit within the existing 4 MB limit. Whole replacement includes this history; contribution-only replacement preserves it while changed source applicability becomes unknown. Replacement preview includes applicability history and the resulting scope guard.
