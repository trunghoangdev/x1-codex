# K-01 guide → K-02 workshop preparation

Iteration 138 connects a retained result from one workstream to actual preparation in another. This bounded Knowledge exercise is optional until Maya makes the first guide offer. Earlier independent workshop briefs remain unchanged.

## People and sequence

1. K-01's current guide needs delivery, Maya's receipt and a suitable reassessment, without a pending revision request.
2. Maya offers that exact assessed guide to Leo / K-02-C. State its purpose and preparation scope, then inspect and confirm the frozen source. One pending package at a time; Maya can cancel it.
3. Leo separately records Received, Clarification requested or Declined. A clarification/decline requires a new offer; earlier responses remain immutable.
4. After receipt, Leo assesses applicability to the stated K-02 preparation purpose: Applicable, Needs adaptation or Not applicable, with rationale. Corrections append decisions rather than rewriting them.
5. A current Applicable decision unlocks delivery of a workshop brief. The brief freezes its source handoff, exact guide snapshot and applicability decision. Maya's separate brief receipt and workshop review remain distinct.

Open either person's My Work or K-01/K-02 workstream to inspect the handoff. Shared dependency and assignment views show whether K-02 preparation is waiting for input. The timeline links offer → response → applicability → brief delivery.

## Changed inputs

A newer guide, changed assessment or pending source revision invalidates readiness for new briefs. Previously delivered briefs retain their original source; a received old brief is flagged as historical input for current workshop review. Maya must offer the new suitably assessed guide, and Leo must receive and assess it again before delivering an updated brief. New packages and later negative applicability decisions also block using an earlier readiness decision for a new brief.

This internal preparation handoff does not transfer publication authority, approve the workshop, allocate facilitation or validate real input access. The UI names simulated actors; persona selection is not authentication. No remote messages are sent.

## Retention and limits

At most twenty handoff packages and ten applicability decisions per package are represented. Brief history includes exact dependency references and prefix counts; earlier independent briefs are distinguished from briefs created after the dependency began.

Whole-workspace checkpoint v8 validates frozen source links, response actors, applicability lineage, history prefixes and downstream brief references while reading earlier formats. Contribution-only replacement preserves handoff/brief history and can leave it stale. Invalid recovery leaves current work unchanged. This is one implemented cross-workstream path, not a general workflow graph or production execution engine.
