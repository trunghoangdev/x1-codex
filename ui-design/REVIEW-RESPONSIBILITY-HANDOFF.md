# Review and decision responsibility handoff

Iteration 139 extends exact-package handoff beyond contribution preparation. Three independent responsibilities are represented for each bounded-use material/cycle:

| Responsibility | Initial holder | Separate local recipient | Bounded rights |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Publication review | Sam | Demo publication reviewer | Assess publication scope |
| Authorization and controls | Sam | Demo authorization delegate | Decide bounded use; suspend, resume or revoke the exact cycle |
| Outcome review | Maya | Demo outcome reviewer | Review retained execution and reader evidence |

These recipients are explicit local demo principals, separate from the contributor delegate and authored worker roster. Inspection and principal selection do not authenticate anyone, provision access or verify capacity. Editorial receipt/reassessment and adopted-scope applicability retain their existing responsibilities.

## Walkthrough

1. Open Knowledge's bounded-use view after allocating a mandate. In **Review and decision responsibility handoff**, inspect current holders and select the owner as the demo actor.
2. Select a responsibility and enter the reason. The other eligible role principal is the recipient, so accepted handoffs can also return responsibility to Sam or Maya. Inspect and confirm the exact package: source, audience, mandate, remaining work, rights and retained decisions/evidence.
3. The named recipient inspects the package and acknowledges input, remaining work and rights before accepting. Decline preserves the existing holder. The owner can cancel a pending proposal. There is at most one pending review proposal per cycle.
4. A proposal alone changes no ownership. After acceptance, shared progress and the local My Work inbox follow the recipient. Select that acting decision principal to record the next decision; former holders and principals from other roles are blocked in both UI and model.
5. For a transferred authorization responsibility, select the current acting authority principal before recording a control decision. Existing grants retain their original author; handoff itself creates no grant and does not restore suspended or revoked authority.

If source, execution, reader evidence, a decision or authority controls change before acceptance, the frozen package is no longer current. Acceptance is blocked; the owner must cancel it and propose the current package. Until acceptance, the original holder can still perform their responsibility, which can itself make the pending package stale. Completed decision stages and terminal authorization cannot be transferred as pending work. Historical outcome review remains available after a revoked grant if execution already occurred.

## History and boundaries

Earlier decisions and their actors never change. Every proposal and response retains its exact work anchor, authority-control count, actor, rationale and package. The Knowledge timeline includes these records. Whole-workspace checkpoint v9 replays handoffs between anchored work/control records and validates recipient, acknowledgment, rights, ownership and subsequent decision actors. Earlier formats remain readable; replacement previews identify incoming decision holders.

New material mandates and new use cycles start with their explicit initial responsibility arrangement. Accepted or pending handoffs from older material/cycles stay historical and do not automatically grant rights in the new cycle. Contribution-only recovery preserves use-role history and may leave its source stale.

At most twenty proposals / forty proposal-response events per cycle are supported. This is a local UI exercise, without production membership, effective permissions, messages, execution or a general delegation service.
