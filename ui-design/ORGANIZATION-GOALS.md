# Organization goals and evidence · iteration 150

The Knowledge overview now connects an explicitly authored organization goal to its two workstreams: **Help members turn shared knowledge into practical next steps**. K-01 contributes reliable answers and next steps through the welcome guide; K-02 contributes practical application through the member workshop.

Open **Organization → Review organization goals and evidence**, or `/organizations/knowledge/goals?persona=leo`. Workstream and outcome pages also link to the shared goal view. Evidence-work and outcome-criteria links retain persona and contextual return.

## Reading the goal map

The overview summarizes how many linked workstreams have a current positive scoped decision in simulation. This is a count of represented decisions, not a completion percentage, organizational health score or proof of real member benefit. Even two positive results do not automatically establish an organization-wide outcome.

Each workstream shows its contribution, expected evidence, current scoped conclusion, exact material/audience/cycle, available evidence with record IDs/actors/times, remaining gaps, retained prior-cycle context and next operational responsibility. Details expose the retained source and evidence records. If follow-up is unallocated, the view says so instead of inventing an owner.

K-01 distinguishes execution, reader evidence, criterion assessment and the separate scoped goal decision. A positive outcome alone leaves the goal decision pending. An accepted/completed follow-up task does not automatically change that goal decision. K-02 distinguishes execution, observations and independent criterion review; a received brief, closed case or preparation-ready decision is not evidence that participants learned.

The organization-level goal owner remains explicitly unallocated in this sample. A local use owner or workshop reviewer is not silently promoted to organization-wide accountability. Assigning that accountability and recording an organization-level assessment are not implemented in this increment.

## Source and cycle boundaries

Changed exact guide/brief/case sources make retained decisions historical for the current goal view. Evidence remains inspectable and does not transfer to a replacement source. New use/workshop cycles begin without inheriting prior positive decisions; previous material and cycle records remain separate. Workshop audience stays in the retained brief rather than being invented as a structured field.

The goal map is independently authored guidance (`goal-map-v1`), not extracted from private material or inferred from activity. Only Knowledge has this explicit map; other organizations remain unchanged. The model reads existing use/goal, case and workshop state without creating decisions, assignments or authority.

No mutable state or new checkpoint format is introduced. Restored v13 (or earlier compatible) workspace records produce the same derived goal/evidence view. No real readership, attendance or learning evidence has been verified.

## Validation

Model checks distinguish criterion versus goal decisions, scoped versus organization-wide results, historical sources, new cycles, completed follow-up and absent evidence. Workspace round-trip preserves the derived view. Phone/desktop browser checks cover the overview entry, readable evidence, scope limits, affected-work/outcome navigation, persona/return continuity and narrow-screen layout. Existing overview and goal-loop checks remain passing; these are technical checks, not participant usability evidence.
