# Suspend and revoke local use authority

Iteration 137 adds Sam's separate control decisions to an Allowed authorization. The original grant and all observations remain immutable. Controls refer to the exact authorization, preceding control and last recorded use stage.

- **Suspend** blocks a future execution observation until Sam records **Resume**. State why use must stop and the conditions needed to resume.
- **Resume** records the verification and rationale for resolving a suspension. The UI also requires the current exact suitable material and applicable adopted scope. It cannot resume revoked authority.
- **Revoke** permanently ends authority for that cycle. It can follow an active grant or suspension. Same-material continuation cannot bypass it; a later suitably assessed material can receive a fresh mandate.

After execution, control decisions preserve the attempt. Reader observations and outcome review can still describe the historical attempt; stopping use does not erase consequences. The exercise does not simulate another execution under the same cycle.

Open the bounded-use view, inspect Use authority, select a decision and enter rationale and conditions or verification. Review the exact authorized source and audience, then explicitly confirm. Cancellation leaves authority unchanged. Maximum twenty control decisions per cycle.

The Knowledge timeline includes all decisions. Whole-workspace checkpoint v7 replays controls between their anchored use stages and rejects invalid links, actors, transitions and overwritten records. Earlier formats remain readable. Prior material and cycle history retain their controls.

All controls are local demo actions by the named simulated authorizer. No production permission, remote job cancellation or participant verification is claimed.
