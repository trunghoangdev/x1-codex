# Workspace layout · iteration 153

Organization now opens with its purpose, linked goals, workstream context and coordination needs. Operational panels no longer precede the overview. The Knowledge overview has a personal inbox entrance, a timeline shortcut and an **Open operating tools** shortcut that expands the tools and moves keyboard focus to their summary.

Allocation, handoff, bounded-use, workshop, brief-input, source-impact, pattern and exception panels remain together inside **Operating tools and local records**, collapsed on the overview. Pending follow-up remains represented in the main Attention and coordination signals. Opening a disclosure changes presentation only; it performs no work or decision.

My Work and operational detail pages show their main content before **Current work actions**. These actions stay expanded. Whole-workspace and contribution recovery stay at the end with their existing individual disclosures. Sample boundary information is also at the end. The layout does not alter checkpoint data, actor responsibilities or operational transitions.

The main software workspace retains its existing layout. This change rearranges the organization scenario workspace; it does not redesign every individual form or introduce a production navigation contract.
