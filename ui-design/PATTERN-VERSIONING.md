Knowledge workstreams now have explicit local operating-pattern selection and version associations for represented work. Open **Workflow → Inspect operating pattern and policy**, or `/organizations/knowledge/patterns/K-01?persona=leo` and the equivalent K-02 route.

## Select guidance and associate work

1. Compare pattern-v1 with pattern-v2. The original authored v1 remains unchanged. Guide v2 adds explicit scope-change, reader/goal-follow-up and changed-review-package expectations; workshop v2 adds separate facilitator acceptance, readiness, execution, observations and repeated-cycle expectations. Decision/escalation policy remains unknown.
2. Select **Demo organization owner**, **Adopt guidance**, the version and a compatibility rationale. Review the exact frozen record and confirm. Observer mode is read-only; actor selection is a demo control, not authentication.
3. Select **Associate work** and the currently adopted version. Choose a represented delivery, bounded-use cycle, workshop brief or workshop-delivery cycle, explain the association, review and confirm.
4. Inspect **Work and retained version**. Every represented work record is explicitly associated or shows **No pattern version recorded**. Missing links do not inherit the selected guidance automatically.
5. To upgrade or roll back, record another guidance selection with rationale. Existing associations retain their version. To follow different guidance for a repeated operation, create a fresh use/workshop cycle and explicitly associate it; existing work cannot be reassigned in place.

The page’s full responsibilities/exchange guidance follows the latest adopted version. A separate comparison disclosure previews the proposed version before selection. History preserves exact pattern content, actor, reason, time, superseded selection, adoption reference and frozen work/context.

## Operational meaning

Selection establishes advisory workstream guidance. Association records which guidance is explicitly associated with a named work source from that point onward. Neither step retroactively certifies prior work, alters operational gates, allocates a worker, grants authority or executes a step. The prototype does not claim that a pattern is an executable workflow engine.

Running workshop/use cycles retain their recorded association through guidance upgrades. Their existing acceptance, source, readiness, authorization and outcome requirements remain independent. Newly created cycles require fresh operational decisions and a separate pattern association. Closed/historical records may be associated as an explicit contextual record; this never certifies their earlier execution.

Delivery identity excludes later receipt changes, and cycle identity excludes later result records, so ordinary progress does not silently detach a version. A replaced/absent source keeps its earlier association in history, while a different source with the same displayed ID receives no inherited association. Contribution-only import preserves pattern history; full workspace replacement explicitly replaces it.

Shared workstream/workflow/use/workshop summaries show selected guidance and association coverage. The Source and scope impact report identifies unassociated work or retained different versions. These are advisory review/historical conditions, not execution blocks. Timeline includes an **Operating pattern** filter.

The two independently authored version libraries are sample guidance, not a remotely managed catalog, rollout service or production policy.
