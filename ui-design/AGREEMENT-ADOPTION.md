# Local workstream agreement adoption

Knowledge K-01 agreement comparison now supports a distinct local adoption decision. Open the agreement as Leo, choose brief-v1 or brief-v2, name the internal cohort or teams and enter a rationale. Prepare, inspect and confirm the decision; cancel changes nothing. Leo is the explicitly simulated workstream coordinator, not a publication approver or authenticated production principal.

Adoption records contain version, audience boundary, rationale, coordinator, timestamp, stream and superseded decision ID. Viewing a newer proposal does not replace adopted scope. Adopting a different version appends history and keeps the earlier record intact. Repeated adoption of the current version is prevented. Editing an already adopted version's audience is outside this bounded exercise; a future agreement revision would require a new decision.

Impact inspection names distribution-scope responsibility K-01-P, editorial criteria K-01-E, researcher inputs K-01-D, contribution and assessment context K-01-H and outcome criterion K-01-goal. It links each source and explains the checks needed for an audience change. These are authored impact checks, not established applicability mappings. Existing exact contribution versions, receipts and assessments remain unchanged. Reader evidence is absent; other evidence is never automatically invalidated. Publication responsibility and authority remain unresolved.

A shared Knowledge banner displays adopted version and audience and links back to impact inspection. Selecting versions and changing adoption do not rewrite assignment inputs, complete work or establish outcome success. Production permission enforcement and explicit record-by-record applicability decisions are not implemented.

State survives app navigation but clears on reload. Main demo snapshots and Knowledge contribution checkpoints exclude adoption history; this boundary appears next to the controls.

Validation: adoption model rejects empty scope, foreign versions and duplicate current-version decisions; keeps superseded history. Browser checks cover prepare/cancel/confirm, focus, proposal/adoption separation, impact/source navigation, history, reload and phone/desktop layout. Existing agreement route/comparison/source-return checks also pass. No participant session or backend result is claimed.
