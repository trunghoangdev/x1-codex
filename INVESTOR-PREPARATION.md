# Investor preparation

The [investment thesis](investor-thesis.md) leads with the completed product and a proposed commercial strategy. This working checklist records the evidence required to turn it into a fundraising offer. It is not part of the opening pitch.

## Founder inputs to supply

| Input | Why an investor needs it |
| --- | --- |
| Company, founders/team, relevant experience and IP ownership | Who can execute and what the company owns |
| Attributable external customer conversations, pilots, repeat use and revenue | Evidence of demand; distinguish conversations from commitments and paid contracts |
| Target buyer, procurement path and one recurring responsibility | A specific entry market and reason to buy |
| Current competitive/market research | Support the timing/category thesis and explain customer alternatives |
| Pricing hypothesis and full delivery/support cost | A commercial model beyond the architecture |
| Raise amount, staffing/infrastructure budget, runway and allocation | A concrete funding request tied to achievable milestones |

Do not invent these inputs. Technical activity, internal use and prototype tests do not establish customer traction or willingness to pay.

## How to present

Use the thesis as the narrative backbone: ambition → customer problem → product → entry buyer → paid expansion → potential advantage → evidence → investment milestones. A deck can use one slide for each major argument, with an organization/contribution visual from the labeled prototype in the product section.

Replace general examples with attributable customer evidence when available. Pair each important claim with its basis. Keep detailed protocol/architecture material in supporting discussion unless it explains a customer benefit or a defensible commercial mechanism.

Before circulation as a funding offer, add the founder/team case and budgeted ask. The current memo is a product/business thesis, not a complete fundraising package. Claims about market size, competitors, current adoption or economic improvements require research and actual measurements.
