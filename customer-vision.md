# Forge — Build the organization your ambition needs

**A product vision for teams that want people, AI and tools to work together as an accountable organization.**

Your ambitions can grow faster than your team's capacity. Serving more customers, improving your product or launching a new operation requires more than producing additional output. Someone must own each responsibility, coordinate the work, check the result and decide what may reach the world.

Our vision for Forge is that you can build that working organization around your goals, combining human expertise with AI and tools. Expand the work you can take on while keeping responsibility and consequential decisions explicit.

## What you would operate

A virtual organization would have its own purpose, roles, assignments, authority, work products and history. People, AI and deterministic tools would fill suitable responsibilities. The organization would continue across attempts, revisions and changes of performer; a conversation ending would not end the work.

In the intended completed experience, you could:

- **Define the organization around an outcome.** Establish its purpose, responsibilities, expected results and rules for deciding whether work is acceptable.
- **Bring the right participants into each responsibility.** Combine your people with compatible AI workers and tools. Keep roles independent from a particular AI vendor or runtime.
- **Give workers room to act.** Delegate meaningful objectives with context, permitted scope and evidence requirements, letting the performer choose how to accomplish the work.
- **Coordinate several streams of work.** See dependencies, handoffs, unresolved responsibilities and where a person needs to contribute or decide. Independent work can progress in parallel.
- **Participate directly.** Each person has a personal work entrance for contributions, assessment, authority decisions and unresolved questions, connected to the shared organizational picture.
- **Improve results through review and revision.** Keep earlier versions and decisions inspectable rather than overwriting them when work is retried.
- **Control consequential actions.** Apply the authority appropriate to each scope. Distinguish a proposed result, an assessment, permission to act and evidence that the action actually happened.
- **Inspect the organization over time.** Understand which inputs and decisions supported a result, what failed and what remains uncertain.

These describe the intended product experience, not capabilities all available today. The organization defines responsibilities and relationships; its work need not follow one fixed pipeline.

## A team that can grow beyond its headcount

Consider a software team supporting an expanding product. People clarify customer needs and set priorities. AI workers prepare scoped changes. Tools run checks. Reviewers assess the exact results, request revisions where needed and decide what may be used. Operations participants inspect effects and unresolved outcomes.

Several improvements can move at once. The organization retains its responsibilities when a worker changes. People can see the whole picture or enter through their own work, without every handoff depending on one person's chat history.

The same organizational grammar could support other domains, with their own work contracts, expertise, policies and evidence. Each application must be developed and validated; software is our first practical proving ground.

## The value we intend to deliver

More capacity from the people and tools you already have. Less repeated coordination. Clearer ownership. Better continuity through failed attempts and revisions. Decisions grounded in inspectable work rather than a performer's claim that it is finished.

Those benefits must be demonstrated on your work. A useful evaluation includes accepted output, human review effort, unsuccessful attempts, total cost and the reliability of continuation. Adding AI workers only helps if the organization delivers results worth accepting.

## What exists, and what comes next

DartMesh defines the reference architecture. Forge implements platform foundations for governed work, execution boundaries and evidence. Software Factory has carried real internal software work. Our organization-centered UI demonstrates the intended experience, with fictional interactive scenarios and separately labeled real retained metadata.

The complete operating experience, live application integration and commercial deployment offering are still being developed. Current internal production work includes operator-led assessment, approval and repository changes. A managed service, fleet operations and new domain packages are future possibilities to validate, not current offerings.

## Start with an outcome that matters

Bring one recurring responsibility that is holding your team back. Define the output, participants, decision rights and acceptance criteria. Demonstrate one complete work path, measure the value, then expand only where the results justify it.
